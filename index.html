Week 1 • Day 1 (Sept 1)

The Genesis of American Letters

English 11: American Literature & Cultural Analysis

Lesson Focus & Objectives

Welcome to English 11! Today we begin our journey into American literary history by examining indigenous oral narratives and exploring how origin stories shape communal identities.

Primary Focus

Constructing a conceptual framework for "American Identity" through primitive indigenous oral narratives.

Core Objective

Evaluating cosmogonic myths to extract underlying communal principles, ecological ties, and societal ethics.

Day One Class Agenda

10 Mins
Course Orientation & Framework: Defining "American Identity" and introducing our inquiry framework for the semester.
20 Mins
Interactive Discussion: What is a Cosmogonic Myth? Deconstructing cultural origin stories.
15 Mins
Textual Close Reading: Introduction to Ojibwe & Iroquois oral storytelling traditions.

💬 Day 1 Reflection Prompt

"How do origin stories reflect a culture's relationship with nature and authority?"

Be prepared to share one takeaway during our opening seminar tomorrow.
