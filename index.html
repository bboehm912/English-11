Day 1: Journalistic Lens & Observational Analysis

Welcome to English 11! Today we merge journalistic observation with literary analysis, establishing our analytical toolkit for examining texts, primary sources, and cultural narratives.

Primary Focus: Developing a disciplined observational habit to analyze texts and artifacts before jumping to conclusions.
Core Objective: Master the OTIS Slow-Looking Framework through interactive media exercises.

The OTIS Slow-Looking Framework

Named in honor of my dog, Otis • Adapted from Dr. Jennifer Holberg's slow-looking pedagogy.

Before jumping to quick conclusions, we use the OTIS Framework to slow down, resist snap judgments, and thoroughly examine literary and visual media through a journalistic lens.

O Observe

Paws & Pause: What textual, structural, or visual details do you notice first before making assumptions?

T Trace

Sniff Out Context: Who created this piece? What historical, cultural, or journalistic framing surrounds it?

I Interpret

Dig Deeper: How do specific word choices, motifs, or narrative structures reveal underlying meaning?

S Synthesize

Fetch the Big Picture: How does this work shape our understanding of broader themes and culture?
